# BRIEF — Notion Pilot · the team

- **workflow:** product-launch-video
- **flow:** autonomous
- **destination:** embedded on the notion-pilot landing page, below the Claudeforce section — 16:9, muted autoplay loop, no audio track
- **length:** 19s

## Intent

Show the multiplayer case. One person dictating notes is a convenience; three people
dictating into one relational database is a CRM that is actually current at 14:21 on a
Tuesday. Then close the governance loop: shared board, separate permissions.

## Angle

The chat thread and the Activities table side by side, in the same order, so the
causality is unmistakable — then the board everybody reads, with each card carrying
the person whose message produced it.
